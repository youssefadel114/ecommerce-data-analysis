# E-Commerce Data Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project explores e-commerce data to understand sales performance, product performance, customer behavior, and order trends. The goal is to turn raw data into meaningful insights that can support better business decisions.

## Objectives
- Analyze revenue and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Explore customer distribution by country.
- Analyze customer spending and average order value.
- Examine order statuses and sales trends over time.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and charts
- KPI analysis
- Data visualization

## Key Insights
- Strong revenue growth: The annual revenue breakdown rises from approximately 354K in 2019 to 8.69M in 2023. The latest year is substantially higher, although the completeness of each year's data should be checked before interpreting this as growth.
- High reported profit margin: Total profit divided by total revenue is approximately 51.9%, based on the workbook's summary figures.
- Leading product departments: Jeans generated approximately 1.57M in revenue, followed by Outerwear & Coats at 1.48M.
- Customer concentration: The United States is identified as the leading country by customer count.
- Order status analysis: Shipped orders account for about 29.9% of the status distribution, followed by completed orders at 25.2%. This can help identify opportunities to improve the customer journey.
